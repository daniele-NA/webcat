2. What Remocat does

You register your face once. From then on, when someone fails to unlock your device, Remocat takes a photo with the front camera, compares the face with yours on the device, and if it is a stranger it saves the photo as a report.

The comparison runs entirely on your device with a local TensorFlow Lite model, downloaded from our servers the first time. Remocat is not a replacement for your device lock: it observes unlock attempts, it does not unlock anything.

3. What the app needs

  • A Google account: to sign in and to save the reports in your own Google Drive.
  • The camera: to register your face and to photograph whoever tries to unlock the device.
  • The Accessibility Service: the only way Android lets an app know about a failed unlock attempt. Details on the dedicated page.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a report has been saved. You can refuse them; monitoring keeps working.

4. Your data

Your registered face never leaves the device: it is converted into a numeric representation and kept encrypted on the phone. The reports go to your own Google Drive, not to us. What reaches our servers is only your email address, your Google account identifier and a device identifier. The full explanation is in the Privacy Policy.
