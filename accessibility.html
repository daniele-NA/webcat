Accessibility Service

Why do we need Accessibility Service?

Remocat requires the Accessibility Service permission to monitor failed unlock attempts on your device. This allows the app to detect unauthorized access attempts and automatically take a photo of the person trying to unlock your device.

What data is collected?

  • No personal data or images are shared or uploaded anywhere.
  • Photos are stored only locally on your device for your review.

How does Remocat respect your privacy?

  • The Accessibility Service is used solely for security monitoring related to device unlock attempts.
  • No sensitive information beyond unlock attempt events and local photos is accessed or transmitted.
  • The app complies with Google Play policies and ensures that permissions are used responsibly and transparently.

How to enable Accessibility Service?

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Accessibility.
  3. Find and select "Remocat Accessibility Service".
  4. Enable it by toggling the switch.

By enabling this permission, you allow Remocat to protect your device by monitoring and recording unauthorized unlock attempts in full respect of your privacy.
